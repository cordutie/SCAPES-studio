SCAPES Studio

Two ways to play with SCAPES, a generative model of environmental sounds. Choose a mode.

Live

real time · best run locally

Play the model like an instrument. Drop semantic sources on a plane and move a point between them: the sound is generated continuously, a fraction of a second at a time, and follows your hand.

  • semantic sources, an atlas of the data and two playgrounds (meaning and structure)
  • effects, level meter and a recorder, all in the browser
  • needs a GPU that keeps up with the sound

Score

offline · timeline · under construction

Compose with the same sources along a timeline, generate the whole piece at once and listen to it afterwards. It does not need to keep up with real time, so it also works on a shared GPU.

  • arrange sources over time
  • render, listen, export
  • coming soon

Code: SCAPES-studio · model: SCAPES · models: SCAPES-models

Live mode on a shared GPU

This page is running on Hugging Face. Free Spaces share their GPUs (ZeroGPU): the GPU is lent in short turns with a time limit, and Live mode needs it all the time. Expect the sound to stop suddenly, to stutter, or not to start at all.

For Live mode as it is meant to be played, run it on your own machine with an NVIDIA GPU: instructions.
